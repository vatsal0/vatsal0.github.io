I just saw a clip of an interview with Jalen Brunson, who just led the New York Knicks to the NBA Finals and a chance at the championship. It was mentioned how he refused to partake in a preemptive photo op with the Finals trophy. I love that mentality. There's nothing inherently wrong with the photo op; it just feels 100x better to hold the prize knowing its actually yours to keep.
